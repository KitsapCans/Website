Questions

good to know.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave your cans at the curb like you normally do on pickup day. We clean them after the truck empties them, then bring them back down your driveway for you.

Which day will you come?

On your regular garbage pickup day. We plan our routes around Bainbridge Disposal's schedule, so your cans are already empty and out.

What do you do to each can?

Five steps, every visit: a sodium hypochlorite (SH) treatment to kill bacteria, a scrub down inside and out, a pressure wash inside and out, a dry, and a deodorizer so it smells good after. Then we bring it back down your driveway.

Can you clean my recycling can? Bainbridge Disposal owns it.

Yes. Signing up gives us your permission to clean every can you list, including the recycling and yard-waste cans the hauler provides.

What happens to the dirty water?

We catch all of it. Washington rules say mobile washers can't let wash water reach the ground, storm drains or surface water, so we collect it on our rig and take it to an approved disposal site.

How do I pay?

You pay per can, per visit. With a card on file we charge it after each visit; otherwise we send you an invoice, due within 10 days.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes, any time. Just let us know at least 48 hours before your next visit. There's no contract.

Why is the monthly plan cheaper?

Regular visits keep grime from building up, and they let us plan efficient routes on each street. We pass that on to you: a monthly clean costs up to 33% less per can than a one-time clean, and a quarterly clean up to 11% less.
