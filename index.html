Questions

good to know.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave your cans at the curb like you normally do on pickup day. We clean them after the truck empties them and leave them where we found them.

Which day will you come?

On your regular garbage pickup day. We plan our routes around Bainbridge Disposal's schedule, so your cans are already empty and out.

Can you clean my recycling can? Bainbridge Disposal owns it.

Yes. Signing up gives us your permission to clean every can you list, including the recycling and yard-waste cans the hauler provides. We use cleaners that are safe for the cans.

What happens to the dirty water?

We catch all of it. Washington rules say mobile washers can't let wash water reach the ground, storm drains or surface water, so we collect it on our rig and take it to an approved disposal site.

Why is monthly so much cheaper?

Regular visits keep grime from building up, and they let us plan efficient routes on each street. We pass that on to you, so a monthly clean costs up to 44% less than a one-time clean.
