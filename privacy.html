privacy policy.

Last updated October 14, 2026

Kitsap Cans cleans garbage, recycling and compost cans on Bainbridge Island, Washington. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • When you request a quote: your first and last name, service address, phone number, and the cans and plan you chose.
  • When you become a customer: your email address if you give it to us, your service schedule, notes about access to your cans, and your billing history.
  • When you pay: payments are handled by our payment processor. We do not store your full card number.
  • When you contact us: the messages, calls and emails you send us.

This website does not add advertising trackers or analytics cookies of its own. The quote form is hosted by our customer-management provider and loads that provider's own scripts to work.

How we use it

  • To give you a quote, schedule your cleanings and find your cans.
  • To send service reminders, invoices, receipts and updates.
  • To answer your questions and handle any issues with a cleaning.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Text messages

If you agree to receive texts, Kitsap Cans will text you about your quote, upcoming cleanings, invoices and service updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of buying our service.

We do not share your mobile number or your text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never sold or shared with anyone, except the service providers that send messages for us.

Who else handles your information

We use a small number of service providers to run the business: a customer-management and scheduling system that stores customer records and sends our texts and invoices, a payment processor, and our email provider. The quote form on this website is provided by our customer-management system. These providers only use your information to provide their services to us.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep customer records while you are a customer and for as long as we need them for billing, tax and legal reasons afterward. If you ask for a quote and do not sign up, we delete your request when we no longer need it.

Your choices

  • Ask what information we have about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Stop texts at any time by replying STOP.
  • Stop emails by replying and asking us to.

Contact us at kitsapcans@gmail.com or (206) 312-7948. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

Our service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date.
